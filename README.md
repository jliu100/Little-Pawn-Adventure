# Little Paws Adventure

Create a landscape-only, single-player pet adventure for iOS and Android. Players adopt a kitten or small dog, care for it through feeding, bathing, exercise, and hospital checkups, and guide it through three evolution stages into a human-like companion that retains its animal traits.

The game combines a 3D home and walkable village—with Home, Weapon, Hospital, Food Store, and Adventure locations—with progressively unlocked 2D minigames: Escape Maze, Puzzle, and Find Stars. In-app purchases and ads are planned for later monetization.
